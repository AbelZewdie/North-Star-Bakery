Welcome to Our Corner of the Neighborhood

Good morning, neighbor! North Star Bakery has been filling this block with the scent of freshly baked bread since 2009. We rise before the sun so you can start your day with something warm, wholesome, and made by hand — every single morning.

Whether you stop in for a quick croissant on your commute, pick up a loaf for Sunday dinner, or plan ahead for a special celebration, we are here for you. Our doors are always open to families, regulars, and curious newcomers alike.

Everything we bake starts with honest ingredients, real technique, and a genuine love for this community. Welcome to the North Star family.
