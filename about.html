How North Star Began

North Star Bakery opened its doors on a chilly October morning in 2009 when Margaret and Thomas Okafor decided that Maplewood deserved a real neighborhood bakery, the kind where the smell of fresh bread greets you half a block away and the person behind the counter already knows your order. What started as a small corner shop with two ovens and a handwritten menu board has grown into a beloved community institution.

The Okafors drew inspiration from Margaret's grandmother, who ran a small patisserie in Lyon, France, and from Thomas's upbringing in Lagos, Nigeria, where baking was a way of bringing people together for every celebration, large or small. North Star carries both of those traditions forward, honoring old techniques while staying rooted in the rhythms of this particular neighborhood.

Over the years, North Star has fed commuters rushing for the 7:15 train, provided the cake for hundreds of birthday parties, and become the go-to caterer for local school events and farmers market days. Every loaf and every pastry still gets the same attention it did on that very first morning, made by hand, with care, for you.

What Goes Into Every Loaf

We believe that great baking starts long before the oven is turned on. That is why we source our ingredients thoughtfully, working with regional farms and mills whenever possible. Our flours come from Sunrise Heritage Mill in central Minnesota, a family-run operation that stone-grinds heritage wheat varieties with deep flavor and nutritional integrity. Our dairy is sourced from Clover Creek Farm, just 40 miles away, where cows are pasture-raised and milked in small batches.

We do not use artificial preservatives, stabilizers, or shortcuts. Fermentation takes time, and we let it. Our sourdough starter, affectionately named "Bernard", has been alive and active since the bakery opened in 2009. When you taste the depth in our bread, you are tasting years of patience.

  • Stone-ground flour from Sunrise Heritage Mill, Fergus Falls, MN
  • Pasture-raised dairy from Clover Creek Farm, Stillwater, MN
  • Cage-free eggs from Happy Hens Co-op, St. Croix County, WI
  • Seasonal fruit from local growers at the Maplewood Farmers Market
  • Wildflower honey from North Star Hive Collective, Maplewood, MN
  • No artificial preservatives, colorings, or additives ever

The People Behind the Counter

Margaret Okafor — Co-Founder & Head Baker

Margaret trained in Lyon and later in San Francisco before returning to the Midwest to open North Star with her husband Thomas. She oversees all bread production and develops new seasonal recipes throughout the year.

Thomas Okafor — Co-Founder & Operations Manager

Thomas handles the day-to-day operations, community partnerships, and the celebratory cake program. He is the reason your order is always ready exactly when promised and that the coffee is always fresh.

Priya Nair — Pastry Chef

Priya joined North Star in 2015 after completing her pastry arts degree at the Minnesota Culinary Institute. She is the creative force behind the pastry case and the one responsible for our increasingly popular seasonal tart series.
